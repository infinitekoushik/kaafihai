Where a mid-level software engineer keeps the most, city by city

Updated November 2026

Where does a mid-level software engineer end up with the most money after living costs? There are two ways to answer: the most rupees left, or the biggest share of salary kept. They give different winners. The figures are estimates, and income tax is not deducted.

What stands out

  • Bangalore leaves the most rupees: about ₹82,500 a month on a ₹1,22,000 salary and ₹39,500 of living costs.
  • Tirupati leads on share of salary kept at about 72%, with several smaller cities almost level. Bangalore keeps 68%.
  • Mumbai ranks 50 of 50 on share kept (58%), because its costs are the highest of any city here.
  • 6 of the top 10 cities by rupees left are among Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai and Kolkata.

How to read this

Rupees left is what you can actually save or spend. Share kept tells you how hard your salary has to work to cover the basics. A higher-paying metro tends to win the first, and a cheaper city tends to win the second. Neither is better by itself. It depends on whether you value a bigger monthly surplus or a lower cost base, and on career growth, which this table cannot measure.

A caution on the salary side

City salary levels are our estimates. We set them against published ranges, which put tech pay in smaller cities roughly 30 to 40 percent below the big metros, but real offers vary widely by company. If you have a real offer in a smaller city, enter it in the calculator instead of relying on this table.

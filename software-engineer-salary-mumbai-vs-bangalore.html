How the costs compare

Rent is the largest difference, at 80% of the monthly gap. A moderate one-bedroom is about ₹28,000 in Mumbai and ₹22,000 in Bangalore. Food is ₹8,000 against ₹7,500, and transport ₹3,500 against ₹3,200. The table shows a moderate lifestyle for one person.

Practical notes

Notes for Mumbai

  • Local trains cover most of the city and cost far less than cabs. A monthly pass is worth buying early.
  • Navi Mumbai is a common way to lower rent. In our estimates, moderate rent there is ₹17,000 against ₹28,000 in Mumbai.
  • Local vegetable markets are usually cheaper than supermarkets and quick-delivery apps for daily produce.

Notes for Bangalore

  • Rent depends heavily on the area. Whitefield and Electronic City are generally cheaper than Indiranagar and Koramangala, but commutes are longer.
  • Namma Yatri, the autos app, is worth trying for short trips.
  • Plan grocery runs ahead. Planned store shopping is usually cheaper than quick-delivery apps.

Things people ask
Which is cheaper, Mumbai or Bangalore?
Bangalore is cheaper in our estimates. A moderate lifestyle costs about ₹39,500 a month there, against ₹47,000 in Mumbai. That is a gap of ₹7,500, or 19% more in Mumbai. Rent makes up 80% of the gap.
What does one person spend each month in Mumbai and Bangalore?
For a moderate lifestyle, about ₹47,000 in Mumbai (rent ₹28,000, food ₹8,000, transport ₹3,500, utilities ₹2,500, other ₹5,000) and about ₹39,500 in Bangalore (rent ₹22,000, food ₹7,500, transport ₹3,200, utilities ₹2,300, other ₹4,500). A frugal lifestyle comes to about ₹25,300 in Mumbai and ₹21,300 in Bangalore.
Does a higher salary make up for higher costs?
At mid level, a Software Engineer in Bangalore has about ₹17,500 more left each month than in Mumbai after moderate living costs, with no income tax deducted. These are estimates for a typical role. Real offers vary a lot, so compare actual numbers.
What does a comfortable lifestyle cost in Mumbai and Bangalore?
For one person, about ₹90,000 a month in Mumbai and ₹77,300 in Bangalore, with rent of ₹55,000 and ₹45,000. Your income after tax has to cover this, plus any savings on top.

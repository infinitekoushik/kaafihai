Cost of living in Mumbai: monthly budget for one person and a family

Updated November 2026

A single person spends about ₹47,000 a month in Mumbai at a moderate lifestyle. That is the 1st most expensive of the 50 cities in the Kaafi Hai model, 115% above the median city. It costs ₹14,700 more than Hyderabad. These are estimates, not survey data.

Common questions about living costs in Mumbai

Is Mumbai expensive to live in?

Among the 50 cities in the Kaafi Hai model, Mumbai is the most expensive at a moderate lifestyle, 115% above the median city. For one person that is about ₹47,000 a month.

How much does a single person need per month in Mumbai?

About ₹25,300 on a frugal budget, ₹47,000 at a moderate one and ₹90,000 at a comfortable one. Rent is the biggest part, 60% of the moderate total.

What does a family of 3 or 4 spend per month in Mumbai?

At a moderate lifestyle, about ₹84,600 for a family of 3 and ₹98,700 for a family of 4 or more. A family of 3 needs about ₹45,540 on a frugal budget and ₹1,62,000 on a comfortable one.

How much salary do I need to live in Mumbai?

For one person at a moderate lifestyle, about ₹58,750 a month before tax keeps 20% of pay, and about ₹67,143 keeps 30%.

Is 10 LPA enough in Mumbai?

10 LPA is about ₹83,333 a month before tax. A single person at a moderate lifestyle spends about ₹47,000 in Mumbai, so that pay is about 1.8 times the cost. Tax and other deductions will lower your in-hand pay, so check your offer letter.

What this leaves out

Rent is a city-wide estimate. Figures leave out tax, and family figures use fixed factors, so a real budget will differ.

How the costs compare

Rent is the largest difference, at 69% of the monthly gap. A moderate one-bedroom is about ₹22,000 in Bangalore and ₹17,000 in Hyderabad. Food is ₹7,500 against ₹6,500, and transport ₹3,200 against ₹2,800. The table shows a moderate lifestyle for one person.

Practical notes

Notes for Bangalore

  • Rent depends heavily on the area. Whitefield and Electronic City are generally cheaper than Indiranagar and Koramangala, but commutes are longer.
  • Namma Yatri, the autos app, is worth trying for short trips.
  • Plan grocery runs ahead. Planned store shopping is usually cheaper than quick-delivery apps.

Notes for Hyderabad

  • In our estimates, rent here is lower than in Bangalore, Delhi or Mumbai. Miyapur and LB Nagar are well-known budget areas.
  • Metro and buses cover a lot of the city. Compare them with app cabs before deciding you need your own vehicle.
  • Weekly markets are worth checking for vegetables and fruit.

Things people ask
Which is cheaper, Bangalore or Hyderabad?
Hyderabad is cheaper in our estimates. A moderate lifestyle costs about ₹32,300 a month there, against ₹39,500 in Bangalore. That is a gap of ₹7,200, or 22% more in Bangalore. Rent makes up 69% of the gap.
What does one person spend each month in Bangalore and Hyderabad?
For a moderate lifestyle, about ₹39,500 in Bangalore (rent ₹22,000, food ₹7,500, transport ₹3,200, utilities ₹2,300, other ₹4,500) and about ₹32,300 in Hyderabad (rent ₹17,000, food ₹6,500, transport ₹2,800, utilities ₹2,000, other ₹4,000). A frugal lifestyle comes to about ₹21,300 in Bangalore and ₹17,000 in Hyderabad.
Which city is better for a career?

What does a comfortable lifestyle cost in Bangalore and Hyderabad?
For one person, about ₹77,300 a month in Bangalore and ₹62,700 in Hyderabad, with rent of ₹45,000 and ₹35,000. Your income after tax has to cover this, plus any savings on top.

Yes. A moderate lifestyle in Bangalore costs around ₹37,000/month vs ₹28,000/month in Hyderabad — roughly 32% more expensive. However, Bangalore salaries in tech are typically 10–15% higher, so the net take-home difference depends on your specific role.
A single person living a moderate lifestyle in Mumbai spends approximately ₹47,000/month — covering rent (₹28,000), food (₹8,000), transport (₹3,500), utilities (₹2,500) and miscellaneous (₹5,000). This varies significantly by neighbourhood.
Among major Indian cities, Bhopal, Indore and Nagpur have the lowest cost of living — a moderate lifestyle costs approximately ₹18,000–20,000/month for a single person. Jaipur and Lucknow are also very affordable.
To live comfortably as a single person in Bangalore, you need at least ₹75,000–80,000/month gross. This covers a good flat (₹45,000), food (₹13,000), transport (₹6,500), utilities (₹3,800) and leaves a meaningful monthly savings buffer.
Salary benchmarks are sourced from AmbitionBox, Glassdoor India and LinkedIn Salary Insights (2025–2026 data). Cost of living figures are sourced from Numbeo and community surveys.

How the numbers work

01
50 cities, role-aware salaries
50 roles, 50 cities, three experience levels. A Finance Analyst's Mumbai premium is very different from a Software Engineer's, so each role category has its own city multipliers.
02
What life actually costs
Rent, food, transport and utilities for every city. Frugal, moderate and comfortable tiers, because a studio in Koramangala and a 2BHK in Miyapur are not the same calculation.
03
What is left after life
Gross salary is a vanity metric. What matters is what is in your account on the 30th. We show you both cities side by side so the answer is obvious.
04
No login, no waiting
Pick your role, pick your cities, get your number. No registration, no email, no paywall. Most people check two or three city pairs before they decide.

Monthly cost of living in 50 Indian cities, ranked

Updated November 2026

Every city in the Kaafi Hai calculator, ranked by what one person needs per month for a moderate lifestyle. The figures are our estimates built from rent, food, transport, utilities and other spending. They are not survey data, and rent is the least precise part, so treat the order as a guide, especially where cities sit close together.

What stands out

  • Varanasi and Jabalpur is the cheapest at ₹17,990 a month. Mumbai is the most expensive at ₹47,000, about 2.6 times as much.
  • 34 of the 50 cities come in under ₹25,000 a month.
  • Rent takes between 47% of the moderate budget (Bhopal) and 60% (Mumbai).

What this ranking does not show

A cheap city is only a good deal if the pay holds up. Salaries fall in smaller cities too, usually by less than costs fall for some roles and by more for others. That is why the calculator compares your salary and your costs together. The next post does this for one role.

The numbers are for one person in a mid-range area. A larger household, a very central address or a long commute will change them.

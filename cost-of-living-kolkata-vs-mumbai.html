How the costs compare

Rent is the largest difference, at 71% of the monthly gap. A moderate one-bedroom is about ₹13,000 in Kolkata and ₹28,000 in Mumbai. Food is ₹5,500 against ₹8,000, and transport ₹2,300 against ₹3,500. The table shows a moderate lifestyle for one person.

Practical notes

Notes for Kolkata

  • Among Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai and Kolkata, Kolkata has the lowest estimated monthly cost.
  • Metro and buses keep daily commuting cheap.
  • New Town is a planned area popular with IT workers. Compare its rent with Salt Lake and the older neighbourhoods.

Notes for Mumbai

  • Local trains cover most of the city and cost far less than cabs. A monthly pass is worth buying early.
  • Navi Mumbai is a common way to lower rent. In our estimates, moderate rent there is ₹17,000 against ₹28,000 in Mumbai.
  • Local vegetable markets are usually cheaper than supermarkets and quick-delivery apps for daily produce.

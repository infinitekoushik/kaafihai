How the costs compare

Rent is the largest difference, at 70% of the monthly gap. A moderate one-bedroom is about ₹22,000 in Bangalore and ₹18,000 in Pune. Food is ₹7,500 against ₹7,000, and transport ₹3,200 against ₹2,800. The table shows a moderate lifestyle for one person.

Practical notes

Notes for Bangalore

  • Rent depends heavily on the area. Whitefield and Electronic City are generally cheaper than Indiranagar and Koramangala, but commutes are longer.
  • Namma Yatri, the autos app, is worth trying for short trips.
  • Plan grocery runs ahead. Planned store shopping is usually cheaper than quick-delivery apps.

Notes for Pune

  • Hinjewadi and Wakad suit people who work in the IT parks. Weigh rent against commute time before choosing.
  • PMPML bus passes are an inexpensive way to commute daily.
  • Mandai is a good place to buy daily vegetables.

Things people ask
Which is cheaper, Bangalore or Pune?
Pune is cheaper in our estimates. A moderate lifestyle costs about ₹33,800 a month there, against ₹39,500 in Bangalore. That is a gap of ₹5,700, or 17% more in Bangalore. Rent makes up 70% of the gap.
What does one person spend each month in Bangalore and Pune?
For a moderate lifestyle, about ₹39,500 (rent ₹22,000, food ₹7,500, transport ₹3,200, utilities ₹2,300, other ₹4,500) in Bangalore and about ₹33,800 (rent ₹18,000, food ₹7,000, transport ₹2,800, utilities ₹2,000, other ₹4,000) in Pune. A frugal lifestyle comes to about ₹21,300 in Bangalore and ₹17,800 in Pune.
How much does a family of 3 or 4 spend in Bangalore and Pune?
At a moderate lifestyle, a family of 3 spends about ₹71,100 a month in Bangalore and ₹60,840 a month in Pune. For a family of 4 or more it is about ₹82,950 in Bangalore and ₹70,980 in Pune.
Which city is better for a career?
That depends more on your role and employer than on the city. Salary and cost both change by city, so compare real offers in the calculator, which adjusts pay by role type.
What does a comfortable lifestyle cost in Bangalore and Pune?
For one person, about ₹77,300 a month in Bangalore and ₹65,700 a month in Pune, with rent of ₹45,000 and ₹37,000. Your income after tax has to cover this, plus any savings on top.

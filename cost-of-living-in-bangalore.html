Cost of living in Bangalore: monthly budget

Updated November 2026

A single person spends about ₹39,500 a month in Bangalore at a moderate lifestyle. That is the 2nd most expensive of the 50 cities in the Kaafi Hai model, 81% above the median city. It costs ₹7,200 more than Hyderabad. These are estimates, not survey data.

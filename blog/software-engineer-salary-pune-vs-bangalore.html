Cost of living breakdown: Pune vs Bangalore

The biggest gap between Pune and Bangalore is rent ₹18,000 vs ₹22,000 per month for a moderate one-bedroom. Food costs vary less dramatically (₹7,000 vs ₹7,500), while transport reflects each city's public infrastructure (₹2,800 vs ₹3,200).

Things people ask
Bangalore is more expensive than Pune for a moderate lifestyle — ₹39,500/month vs ₹33,800/month, a difference of ₹5,700 (17%). Rent is the biggest driver: ₹22,000 in Bangalore vs ₹18,000 in Pune.
A single person living a moderate lifestyle in Bangalore spends approximately ₹39,500/month — rent (₹22,000), food (₹7,500), transport (₹3,200), utilities (₹2,300) and miscellaneous (₹4,500). A frugal lifestyle can bring this down to around ₹25,675/month.
Both cities have strong job markets but in different sectors. Bangalore leads in tech and startups. The better city for your career depends more on your specific role and company than city alone — use the calculator above to see what the numbers look like for your situation.
For a comfortable single-person lifestyle in Bangalore, you need approximately ₹55,300/month gross salary — this covers ₹29,700 rent, all expenses and leaves a meaningful savings buffer. For a family of two, budget at least ₹85,912/month.

Data sourced from Numbeo, AmbitionBox, Glassdoor India and LinkedIn Salary Insights (2025–2026). Figures represent moderate lifestyle costs for a single person in a mid-range neighbourhood. Actual costs vary by specific location, company and individual spending patterns.

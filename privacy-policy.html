Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Kaafi Hai ("we", "us", or "our") operates kaafihai.in. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it and your rights regarding your data.

Information we collect

Kaafi Hai is designed to work without requiring any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address or any other identifying information to use the calculator.

We may automatically collect the following non-personal information when you visit our site:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website (how you found us)
  • General geographic location (country or city level, not precise)

This data is collected via Google Analytics and is used only in aggregate form to understand how the site is used and how to improve it.

Cookies

We use the following cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Helps us understand visitor behaviour in aggregate. You can opt out via Google's opt-out tool.
  • Google AdSense: Used to display relevant advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site or other sites. You can opt out at Google Ad Settings.
  • Preference cookies: We store your dark/light mode preference in localStorage so the site remembers your choice.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

We also include affiliate links to third-party services (such as Naukri, Amazon and NoBroker). If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase or sign up, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only link to services we believe are relevant and useful.

How we use your information

  • To understand how visitors use the site and improve it
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense
  • To process feedback you voluntarily submit via our feedback form

Data sharing

We do not sell, trade or transfer your personal information to third parties. Aggregate, anonymised analytics data may be processed by Google Analytics. Advertising data is processed by Google AdSense. We do not share individual user data with any other parties.

Data retention

We do not store personal data on our servers. Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics per their own retention policies. Any feedback you submit via email is retained only as long as needed to respond to your query.

Your rights

You have the right to opt out of Google Analytics tracking at any time using this browser add-on. You can clear localStorage data (including your theme preference) by clearing your browser's site data for kaafihai.in.

Children's privacy

Kaafi Hai is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@kaafihai.in.

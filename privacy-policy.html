Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains what Kaafi Hai collects, what it does with it and the choices you have. In short, the calculator works without an account, and we do not ask for your name or email to use it.

What we collect

The calculator runs in your browser. The city, role and salary you enter are not sent to our servers.

When you calculate, the page address is updated with your choices, including salary, so you can bookmark or share the result. Anyone you send that link to can see those values.

Like any website, our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical data such as IP address and browser type to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Saved in your browser

We save your language and light or dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage (kh-lang and kh-theme) so the site remembers it. This stays on your device.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It can collect pages viewed, device and browser type, and approximate location. We do not use it to identify you. You can opt out with Google’s browser add-on.

Ads and cookies

We may show ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalised ads in Google Ad Settings or opt out at aboutads.info. Google explains how it uses data from sites like this on its partner sites page.

Affiliate links

Some links lead to partners such as Naukri, NoBroker and Amazon. When you click one, the partner or an affiliate network such as Cuelinks may set a cookie to record that the visit came from us, so that we can earn a commission. We do not receive your personal details from these partners.

Fonts

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers when a page opens.

Messages you send us

If you email us or use the contact form, which opens your email app, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the site.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with the services above and where the law requires it. Emails are kept as long as needed to deal with your message. Analytics data follows Google’s retention settings.

Your choices

You can clear the site’s data in your browser to remove saved preferences, block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of personalised ads using the links above. To ask about or delete an email you sent us, write to hello@kaafihai.in.

Children

Kaafi Hai is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@kaafihai.in.

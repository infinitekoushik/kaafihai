How the costs compare

Rent is the largest difference, at 61% of the monthly gap. A moderate one-bedroom is about ₹20,000 in Delhi and ₹22,000 in Bangalore. Food is ₹7,000 against ₹7,500, and transport ₹3,000 against ₹3,200. The table shows a moderate lifestyle for one person.

Practical notes

Notes for Delhi

  • A metro smart card covers most daily travel, and over a year the saving compared with cabs adds up.
  • Noida is a common way to lower rent. In our estimates, moderate rent there is ₹16,000 against ₹20,000 in Delhi.
  • Sarojini Nagar and Lajpat Nagar are well known for budget shopping.

Notes for Bangalore

  • Rent depends heavily on the area. Whitefield and Electronic City are generally cheaper than Indiranagar and Koramangala, but commutes are longer.
  • Namma Yatri, the autos app, is worth trying for short trips.
  • Plan grocery runs ahead. Planned store shopping is usually cheaper than quick-delivery apps.

Things people ask
Which is cheaper, Delhi or Bangalore?
Delhi is cheaper in our estimates. A moderate lifestyle costs about ₹36,200 a month there, against ₹39,500 in Bangalore. That is a gap of ₹3,300, or 9% more in Bangalore. Rent makes up 61% of the gap.
What does one person spend each month in Delhi and Bangalore?
For a moderate lifestyle, about ₹36,200 in Delhi (rent ₹20,000, food ₹7,000, transport ₹3,000, utilities ₹2,200, other ₹4,000) and about ₹39,500 in Bangalore (rent ₹22,000, food ₹7,500, transport ₹3,200, utilities ₹2,300, other ₹4,500). A frugal lifestyle comes to about ₹18,700 in Delhi and ₹21,300 in Bangalore.
Which city is better for a career?
That depends more on your role and employer than on the city. Salary and cost both change by city, so compare real offers in the calculator, which adjusts pay by role type.
What does a comfortable lifestyle cost in Delhi and Bangalore?
For one person, about ₹72,000 a month in Delhi and ₹77,300 in Bangalore, with rent of ₹42,000 and ₹45,000. Your income after tax has to cover this, plus any savings on top.

Last updated: November 2026

Everything on Kaafi Hai is an estimate for general information. It is not financial, tax, legal or career advice.

How the numbers are made

Salaries: for each role we set a typical monthly figure for junior, mid and senior levels, then adjust it for the city and the type of role. Living costs: for each city we estimate monthly rent, food, transport, utilities and other spending for three lifestyles. These are our own estimates. They are not survey data and are not copied from any single website.

How we checked them

Where public references exist, we compared our estimates against them. These include published salary reports from recruitment and consulting firms (for example Randstad, Taggd and Draup), government pay scales (the 7th Pay Commission and UGC) and published property price reports. Public data does not cover every role in every city, so many figures are still our best judgment. Rent and living costs are the least precise part.

Why real numbers differ

  • Your employer, company size and how your package is structured
  • The neighbourhood within a city
  • Your own spending habits and lifestyle
  • Your experience and skills, and the industry you work in
  • Market changes over time

Salary figures are monthly estimates. Comparisons that subtract living costs do not deduct income tax or other deductions, so real take-home can be lower.

Not advice

Nothing here is financial, legal, tax or career advice. The calculator is a starting point for your own research. Before relocating, accepting an offer or negotiating a salary, talk to a qualified adviser and check current listings.
